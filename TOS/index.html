Privacy Policy

Section 03

3.1 Summary — Minimal Data, Stored Locally

We do not sell your data, run advertising trackers, or collect analytics on your PC. Most launcher settings and logs stay strictly on your own device.

3.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The launcher stores configuration files, cached manifests, downloaded updates, and logs in %APPDATA%\HavocLauncher (or in HavocData\ next to the executable in portable mode):

  • Your chosen profile name/callsign, avatar color/URL, UI theme, language, launch arguments, and game folder path.
  • Local mod installation state (mods_state.json), local launch counter, and diagnostic logs (launcher.log / MelonLoader Latest.log).

3.3 Discord OAuth2 & Rich Presence

  • Discord Sign-In (Optional): Uses Discord OAuth2 with the identify scope only to read your Discord User ID, username/display name, and avatar URL. This is used solely to display your profile in the launcher and check eligibility for Closed Beta / Admin mods. We never access your email, servers, messages, or password.
  • Discord Rich Presence (Optional): Communicates locally with your running Discord desktop client via IPC to display your Havoc Rebirth status. You can turn it off anytime in Settings.

3.4 P2P Anti-Cheat & Lobby Verification Data

Only while running Havoc with EyeOnGame and MakeIt P2P Handshake enabled in a multiplayer lobby, the mods perform local integrity checks and share the verification result directly with your lobby host over the game’s P2P session:

  • SHA-256 hash of GameAssembly.dll and list/digest of .dll files inside the game’s Mods/ folder.
  • Presence of prohibited mod-loader entries in the game root (e.g., BepInEx/proxy DLLs), author.flag, active cinematic Freecam object, modified Time.timeScale, and names of running processes matching known debugging/cheating tools.

This check runs locally in memory and is used strictly for lobby integrity; MakeIt does not upload or store your process list on any central database.

3.5 Network Requests & Third Parties

The launcher and web pages make standard HTTPS requests to fetch the mod manifest, news feed, and updates (mwkdev.pp.ua / GitHub Releases), Steam store header imagery, and Discord API (only if you sign in with Discord). Hosting infrastructure (such as GitHub Pages and CDNs) may log standard HTTP metadata (IP address, user-agent, timestamp) according to their own privacy policies.

3.6 Your Controls & Data Deletion

  • You can sign out of Discord anytime in the Profile tab or revoke the app inside Discord → Settings → Authorized Apps.
  • You can delete all locally saved launcher data anytime by removing %APPDATA%\HavocLauncher (or HavocData\).
  • Questions or deletion requests? Contact MakeIt Squad on Discord or GitHub.
